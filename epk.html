The Artist.

Born in Zimbabwe, matured in the UK, and based in Singapore for over a decade, TAKüRA brings a perspective that is simultaneously global and deeply local. His sets weave deep, soulful Afro House with rich melodic layers, building energy with intention and releasing it at exactly the right moment.

A holder of the WSET Level 2 Award in Wines, TAKüRA understands how to pair sound with a premium experience, a natural fit for members’ clubs, rooftop bars, and luxury brand activations. He has played at 67 Pall Mall Singapore, one of the world’s most prestigious members’ wine clubs, and most recently headlined the Andaz Singapore Gin Launch Party at Mr Stork, Level 39.

As a producer he releases on his own label, Inner Moto: Afro House rooted in the sonic memory of Zimbabwe, with mbira textures and vocals moving between Shona and English.
